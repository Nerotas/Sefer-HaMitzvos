# Unsubscribe and Notification Control

Your calendar and messaging participation is fully opt-in. Here are simple ways to opt-out anytime.

## Google Calendar subscribers

- Google Calendar (web)

  1. Open https://calendar.google.com
  2. Gear → Settings
  3. Under "Settings for my calendars" select "Sefer HaMitzvos Daily Study"
  4. Access permissions / Remove calendar → Unsubscribe

- Google Calendar (mobile app)

  1. Open the app → tap the calendar name
  2. Uncheck the calendar to hide events, or Remove it to unsubscribe

- Apple Calendar / Outlook / other apps (iCal)

  - Remove the subscribed iCal calendar from Accounts/Subscriptions to stop updates

- Manage reminders (optional)
  - In the calendar’s settings you can reduce or disable event notifications without unsubscribing

## WhatsApp daily messages (if you joined)

- Reply with `STOP`
- You’ll receive a confirmation that you’ve been unsubscribed
- You can rejoin later by replying `START` or via the web opt-in page (if provided)

## Revoke Google access (only for people who ran the setup)

If you authorized the app while creating or maintaining the calendar:

1. Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
2. Find "Sefer HaMitzvos Calendar" (or your app name)
3. Click "Remove access"

## Privacy

- We do not sell or share personal data
- Calendar subscriptions are anonymous to us
- WhatsApp opt-in status is used only to deliver your requested messages

If you need help, open an issue in the repository or contact the maintainer.
